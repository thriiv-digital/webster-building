About Webster Building.

AHHA-certified, licensed & insured carpentry specialists with 13+ years of experience in decks, flooring, fencing, and fireplace installations across Shellharbour and the Illawarra.

Meet Lachy Webster.

Webster Building is led by Shellharbour local Lachy Webster. With more than 13 years of industry experience, Lachy brings a wealth of expertise, structural knowledge, and a passion for quality carpentry to every job site.

We specialise in everything from custom decks and pergolas to internal renovations and specialised fireplace installations. Our philosophy is simple: provide homeowners across Shellharbour and the Illawarra with practical advice, premium workmanship, and a straightforward, stress-free building experience.
