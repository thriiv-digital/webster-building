How we work.

A simple, transparent process from your first enquiry through to final handover.

01

Enquire & Initial Quote

Reach out via phone, text, or our online form with a brief outline of your project. If you have photos, plans, or measurements, send them through. We'll discuss your ideas, review your requirements, and provide an initial estimate or arrange a convenient time for an on-site visit.

Contact us →
02

Site Consultation & Detailed Proposal

For most projects, we visit your home across Shellharbour or the Illawarra to inspect the space, discuss design options, measure up, and assess any structural or site-specific needs. We then provide a clear, itemised quote detailing scope, materials, and turnaround time—with zero hidden surprises.

03

Planning & Quality Construction

Once you approve the quote, we schedule your start date, order quality materials from trusted local suppliers, and prepare the site. During construction, we maintain high standards of workmanship, communicate clearly about daily progress, and keep a clean, safe site that respects your home.

04

Final Inspection & Handover

When construction is complete, we walk through the finished project together to ensure every detail meets your expectations and our high standards. We review maintenance tips for your timber, decking, or fireplace, hand over any warranties, and leave your space spotless and ready to enjoy.
