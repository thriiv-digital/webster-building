Fencing in Berry

Around Berry's historic town centre, timber fencing is often the preferred choice to match the area's heritage character, while properties further out on the surrounding farmland have more flexibility and often need longer, paddock-style boundary runs rather than a standard suburban fence line.

We quote both timber and Colorbond fencing and can handle rural-scale jobs alongside standard residential blocks. Standard NSW height rules apply for residential boundaries — generally 1.8m for side and rear boundaries, 1.2m facing the street. Extending or replacing an existing paddock boundary line is one of the more common Berry fencing jobs we take on.

Flooring in Berry

Most of our Berry flooring work is on established or heritage-era homes wanting a partial replacement or full refinish to match existing boards, which takes more care than a straightforward new-build install since matching timber species, board width and finish to a character home's existing flooring is a different task entirely.

Subfloor preparation is a genuine consideration in Berry's older housing stock, where decades of settling and previous repairs can leave an uneven base. We check and prepare the subfloor properly on every job rather than laying straight over a problem that will show up within a year. Timber and engineered timber remain the most popular choices for Berry's heritage-era homes, matching the period character better than a more modern-looking material.

Fireplace installation in Berry

Berry's cooler inland climate and heritage housing stock mean fireplace requests here often involve retrofitting a wood heater or electric fireplace into an older home that was never built with one, working carefully around existing period detailing rather than a blank canvas. As AHHA-certified installers, we work strictly to AS/NZS 2918 clearance and safety standards on every job, whether that's a full masonry chimney retrofit, a zero-clearance unit built into a custom timber-framed surround, or an electric fireplace with no flue requirements at all.
