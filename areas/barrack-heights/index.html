Fireplace installation in Barrack Heights

Most Barrack Heights homes are established rather than new-build, so the majority of our fireplace work here is retrofitting a modern wood heater or electric fireplace into a house that wasn't originally built with one. As AHHA-certified installers, we work strictly to AS/NZS 2918 clearance and safety standards on every job, whether that's a full masonry chimney retrofit, a zero-clearance unit built into a custom timber-framed surround, or an electric fireplace with no flue requirements at all.

Being this close to the coast, we'll also check flue and flashing specifications suit a mild salt-air environment, similar to the consideration we apply on jobs closer to Warilla or Kiama, even though Barrack Heights sees less direct exposure than a suburb right on the ocean.

Fencing in Barrack Heights

Fencing work in Barrack Heights is mostly replacements and repairs on established properties, so matching an existing style or upgrading an ageing fence to something more modern and low-maintenance are the two most common requests.
