From Tongarra Road to the new Calderwood and Tullimbar estates, Webster Building brings licensed, trade-level workmanship to homes right across Albion Park.

Local to Albion Park, based in Shellharbour.

Albion Park sits at the foot of the Illawarra escarpment, a short drive from Shellharbour, where Tongarra Road still runs through a town centre that carries the suburb's older dairy-farming character. That semi-rural feel is changing fast: Calderwood and Tullimbar, two of the largest new residential growth areas in the Illawarra, are steadily filling in the paddocks around Albion Park with new streets and homes, while the older parts of town closer to Tongarra Road and the Illawarra Highway hold onto a more established, acreage-style character.

That mix means Webster Building sees a genuinely wide range of work in Albion Park — from framing and fit-outs in brand-new Calderwood homes, to careful repairs and additions on older properties that have had decades of extensions, sheds and outbuildings added over time. Macquarie Pass and the escarpment foothills are close by too, which means some Albion Park blocks slope more than a typical suburban lot, and we regularly design decks, fencing and framing around that terrain rather than assuming flat ground.

Note that Albion Park and Albion Park Rail are distinct suburbs, even though they sit right next to each other — this page focuses on Albion Park itself, while Albion Park Rail, home to Illawarra Regional Airport, is a separate area we also regularly service. We service both, so if you're not sure which side of the line your property falls on, just ask.

Whether you're finishing a new Calderwood build or maintaining an established Albion Park property, we bring the same trade-level precision and straightforward pricing to every job — and there's no minimum job size, so a single repair is just as welcome as a full renovation.

Fireplace installation in Albion Park

Albion Park's older, more established homes near Tongarra Road often want a wood heater or electric fireplace retrofitted where one was never originally built in, while new Calderwood and Tullimbar builds more commonly specify a fireplace and feature wall as part of the design from the start. Being that bit further from the coast and closer to the escarpment, Albion Park sees genuinely cooler winter nights, which makes a properly sized, correctly installed heater a practical addition rather than just a feature.

As AHHA-certified installers, we work strictly to AS/NZS 2918 clearance and safety standards on every job, whether that's a full masonry chimney retrofit, a zero-clearance wood heater built into a custom timber surround, or an electric fireplace with no flue requirements at all. On larger, older Albion Park properties we're also often asked to build a feature wall or surround that fits a bigger, more open living space than a standard suburban lounge room.

Flooring in Albion Park

New Calderwood and Tullimbar builds usually want timber, hybrid or vinyl flooring installed fresh before move-in, on a clean subfloor with nothing to work around. Established Albion Park homes more commonly need a partial floor replacement or refinish to match existing boards — a job that takes more care, since matching timber species, board width and finish to what's already down the hallway is a different task to fitting out an empty new build.

Subfloor preparation matters in both cases, but it matters more in Albion Park's older housing stock, where decades of moisture, settling or previous repairs can leave an uneven base. We check and prepare the subfloor properly before anything new goes down, rather than laying straight over a problem that will show up within a year.

Fencing in Albion Park

New estate blocks across Calderwood and Tullimbar typically need full boundary fencing installed as a first fit-out, and Colorbond is a common choice there for a consistent, low-maintenance streetscape that meets many estate covenant requirements. Around the older parts of Albion Park, timber fencing remains common, particularly on larger, more established properties where a uniform streetscape matters less than matching what's already there.

Being inland, Albion Park doesn't carry the same salt-air corrosion considerations as Kiama, so material choice tends to come down to budget, maintenance preference and matching your street rather than durability in a harsh coastal environment. We quote both timber and Colorbond and can talk you through standard NSW height rules — generally 1.8m for side and rear boundaries, 1.2m facing the street. For larger acreage-style properties, we also handle longer fence runs and paddock-style boundary fencing, not just standard suburban block lengths.
