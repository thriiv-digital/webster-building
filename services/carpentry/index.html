Residential Carpentry & Repairs.

Structural framing, internal fix-outs, cladding repairs, and emergency timber works—delivered with the same trade-level precision we bring to every major build.

No job too small.

Not every project fits neatly into a major deck build or full floor installation—and that's where our residential carpentry services step in. Whether you need structural framing for a minor room reconfiguration, precision timber fix-outs, or emergency timber repairs, Webster Building delivers the exact same trade-level precision and structural integrity to smaller jobs as we do to complete outdoor builds.

From restoring weathered timber elements to internal architectural detailing, we make sure your home's structural and decorative woodwork is built clean, square, and built to last.

Let's Talk

Structural & framing work

  • Timber Wall & Floor Framing: Precision-installed wall studs, floor joists, and structural sub-framing for room modifications or small home extensions.
  • Wall Removal & Load Support: Skilled structural timber adjustments and beam installations to open up living spaces safely.
  • Carports & Verandahs: Heavy-duty timber framing constructed to withstand the elements and match your home's design.

Internal fix-outs & architectural detailing

  • Doors & Windows: Professional door hanging (internal, external, and sliding barn doors), timber door frame repairs, window surround replacements, and hardware fitting.
  • Skirting Boards & Architraves: Clean, seamless installation and replacement of skirtings, architraves, picture rails, and decorative wall paneling (such as VJ lining boards and dado rails).
  • Stairs, Handrails & Balustrades: Internal timber staircase modifications, tread replacements, handrails, and safety balustrade installations.

Cladding, repairs & emergency works

  • Exterior Cladding & Weatherboards: Installation and repairs for timber weatherboards, eaves, and fascia board replacements.
  • Make-Safes & Structural Repairs: Fast-response emergency make-safes, structural propping, and securing timber structures following storm damage, decay, or impact.
  • Termite & Rot Restoration: Removing and replacing rotted or termite-damaged sub-floor timbers, studs, joists, and structural fascia.
